📊 Team Statistics

Att / Def compare each team to the league average (1.00). Attack above 1 is better than average; defence below 1 means fewer goals conceded than average.

🧠 How the predictions work

  1. Team strength. Each team's goals scored and conceded per game, at home and away, are divided by the league average to give attack and defence ratings. Teams with only a few games are pulled towards average so one big result doesn't skew them.
  2. Expected goals. Home xG = league home average × home attack × away defence (and the reverse for the away side). Recent form (last 5 results) nudges xG by up to ±12%.
  3. Scoreline grid. A Poisson distribution turns each xG into the chance of scoring 0, 1, 2… goals. Every scoreline from 0–0 to 8–8 gets a probability, with a Dixon-Coles correction for the extra draws seen in real football.
  4. Markets. Summing the grid gives home / draw / away, over 2.5 goals, both teams to score, and the most likely exact scores. Confidence is the probability of the top outcome; the rating also rewards a clear gap over the next outcome.

Predictions are statistical estimates, not guarantees. Football is high-variance, and even a 60% favourite loses or draws 4 times in 10. Not betting advice.
